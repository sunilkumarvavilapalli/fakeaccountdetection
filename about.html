About This Project

Understanding the threat of fake accounts and how AI can help detect them

The Problem

Fake social media accounts pose serious threats including misinformation spread, phishing attacks, artificial engagement manipulation, and platform manipulation. These accounts can:

Spread misinformation and false narratives

Manipulate engagement metrics and trends

Launch targeted phishing and scam campaigns

Compromise user privacy and security

Our Solution

We propose a multimodal fake account detection system that combines advanced AI techniques with machine learning to automatically identify suspicious accounts with high accuracy.

Image Analysis

Advanced CLIP model analyzes profile pictures for authenticity

Text Extraction

OCR technology extracts metadata from profile images

ML Classification

Random Forest classifier for accurate detection
